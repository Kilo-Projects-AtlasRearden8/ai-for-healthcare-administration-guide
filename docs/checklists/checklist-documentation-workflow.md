# Checklist: Documentation Workflow

Print this sheet. Check each box as you complete the step. Education only — not
medical advice. The treating clinician reviews and signs every note.

**Clinic:** ____________________   **Clinician:** ____________________   **Date:** __________

## Before using AI

☐ Confirm your clinic allows AI-assisted documentation drafting  
☐ Open a fresh AI chat  
☐ Use fake patient details only in prompts (example: Jordan Example, 05/12/1988)  
☐ Never paste real patient names, MRNs, diagnoses, or visit details into AI  

## Draft with AI

☐ Provide bullet points from the visit (S: subjective, O: objective, A: assessment, P: plan)  
☐ Ask AI to draft SOAP sections or a visit summary in professional language  
☐ Request a draft, not a final note: "Draft for clinician review"  

## Clinician review (required)

☐ Read every line of the AI draft  
☐ Correct anything inaccurate or missing  
☐ Confirm the plan matches the assessment  
☐ Remove any guesses or diagnoses you did not make  
☐ Confirm medications, dates, and details are correct  

## Finalize

☐ Copy approved text into the EHR  
☐ Sign the note yourself — AI cannot sign  
☐ Delete the AI chat if clinic policy requires  
☐ Save any approved templates with version dates
