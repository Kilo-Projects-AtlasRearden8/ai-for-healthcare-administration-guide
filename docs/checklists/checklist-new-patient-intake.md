## Before you start

☐ Confirm your clinic allows new-patient intake forms to be drafted or updated  
☐ Identify who approves forms: compliance officer, legal counsel, clinical lead  
☐ Open a fresh AI chat. Never reuse an old conversation  
☐ Use fake data only in AI prompts (example: Jane Example, 01/01/2000)  
☐ Never paste real patient names, MRNs, insurance IDs, or contact info into AI  

## Draft the form with AI

☐ Ask AI to draft the form in plain English (about 8th-grade reading level)  
☐ Include: name, DOB, phone, email, emergency contact, reason for visit, medications, allergies  
☐ Add HIPAA acknowledgment line (to be worded by compliance)  
☐ Add disclaimer at top: "Draft — review by compliance and counsel before use"  
☐ Rewrite anything awkward in your own words  

## Review and approve

☐ Read the full draft line by line  
☐ Confirm all fields your clinic needs are included  
☐ Send to compliance officer and legal counsel for approval  
☐ Get written approval before printing or using with patients  

## Finalize

☐ Save approved version as master template with version date  
☐ Label: "Approved by compliance on ___"  
☐ Store blank forms securely; staff work from copies only  
☐ Shred drafts and misprints
