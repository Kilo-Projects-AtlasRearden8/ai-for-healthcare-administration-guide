## Review and send

☐ Read every message before sending — fix tone and accuracy  
☐ Add real patient details in your scheduling system, not in the AI chat  
☐ Have a staff member approve and send each reminder  
☐ Log the reminder per clinic policy  

## Reduce no-shows

☐ Send reminders 24–48 hours before the visit  
☐ Offer text, email, or phone reminders by patient preference  
☐ Keep a waitlist so canceled slots can be refilled  
☐ Post the cancellation policy in plain language at check-in
