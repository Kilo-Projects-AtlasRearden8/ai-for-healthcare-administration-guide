## What NOT to do

☐ Do NOT let AI assign CPT, HCPCS, or ICD-10 codes  
☐ Do NOT let AI decide whether a claim should be paid or denied  
☐ Do NOT paste real claims, EOBs, remittance advices, or patient billing details into AI  
☐ Do NOT submit claims based on AI output  

## Review and approve

☐ Have billing manager or certified coder review any superbill or template  
☐ Verify all amounts, dates, and balances before any patient letter goes out  
☐ Confirm code sets are current (they change yearly)  

## Compliance habits

☐ Never upcode or bill for services not performed  
☐ Follow your clinic's compliance plan  
☐ Ask your compliance officer when in doubt
