# Checklist: Quarterly Compliance Review

not legal advice. Your compliance officer has the final word.

## AI and patient data

☐ Confirm no staff are pasting patient data into public AI tools  
☐ Confirm staff use fake examples (Jane Example, 01/01/2000) when practicing with AI  
☐ Verify each AI tool in use is approved by the clinic  
☐ Check whether each vendor signs a BAA; treat tools without a BAA as unsafe for patient-related work  

## HIPAA habits

☐ Staff lock computers when stepping away  
☐ Papers with patient info are shredded, not recycled  
☐ Patient discussions happen privately, not in hallways or waiting areas  
☐ Minimum necessary rule is followed: staff access only what they need  

## Forms and documentation

☐ All patient-facing forms have current compliance and counsel approval  
☐ Forms carry version dates and "Approved by compliance" labels  
☐ Clinicians review and sign every AI-assisted note before it is final  
☐ No AI-generated codes or claims in patient charts  

## Training

☐ New staff trained on AI ground rules during onboarding  
☐ Refresher shared with all staff this quarter  
☐ Everyone knows who the compliance officer is and how to reach them  

## Incidents

☐ Review any reported mistakes or possible exposures from this quarter  
☐ Confirm each was reported to the compliance officer promptly  
☐ Document what changed to prevent repeat issues
