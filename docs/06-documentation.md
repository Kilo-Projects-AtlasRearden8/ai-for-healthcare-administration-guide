# 06 Documentation Workflows

AI can help draft and organize visit documentation — but the clinician who
saw the patient must always review, correct, and sign the final note. AI does
not make clinical decisions.

Education only. Not medical advice. The clinician is responsible for every
note in the chart.

## What AI can help with

- Drafting SOAP note sections from bullet points (clinician reviews and signs)
- Rewriting provider shorthand into complete sentences
- Creating documentation templates for common visit types
- Drafting patient visit summaries and after-visit instructions
- Proofreading notes for clarity and tone

## What AI cannot do

- AI cannot diagnose or decide what went in the note.
- AI cannot bill or code the visit.
- AI cannot sign a note. Only the treating clinician signs.
- Never paste real patient details into an AI tool.

## SOAP note refresher (education only)

| Section | What it includes |
|---------|------------------|
| **S** — Subjective | What the patient reports: symptoms, concerns, history |
| **O** — Objective | What the clinician observes or measures: vitals, exam findings |
| **A** — Assessment | The clinician's clinical judgment about the problem |
| **P** — Plan | What happens next: treatment, follow-up, referrals |

## Step-by-step: AI-assisted documentation draft (fake data only)

Clinician: Dr. Rivera. Fake patient: Jordan Example, DOB 05/12/1988, visit
04/10/2026.

1. The clinician jots down bullet points after the visit (never in the AI tool
   if they contain real patient info).
2. Open a new AI chat.
3. Prompt example:

   > "Draft a SOAP note section from these bullet points. Use fake patient
   > details only. Patient Jordan Example, DOB 05/12/1988, seen 04/10/2026.
   > Bullets: Patient reports lower back pain for 3 days, no injury, rates pain
   > 4/10. Exam: full range of motion, no red flags. Assessment: acute low
   > back strain. Plan: activity modification, heat, ibuprofen per label,
   > return in 2 weeks if not better. Keep it professional and concise."

4. **The clinician reads every line**, corrects anything wrong, and adds what
   is missing. Never trust AI output as-is.
5. The clinician copies the approved text into the EHR and signs the note.
6. Delete the AI chat when done if your clinic policy requires it.

## Documentation templates

Ask AI to draft templates, then have your clinical lead approve them:

- New patient visit template
- Follow-up visit template
- Telehealth visit template
- After-visit summary template

Templates are blank forms — they contain no patient data. AI can help write
the structure; humans approve the content.

## Proofreading tips

- Read the note out loud. Does it sound like what actually happened?
- Check names, dates, and medications carefully — AI can mix these up.
- Make sure the plan matches the assessment.
- Remove anything that sounds like a guess or a diagnosis the clinician did
  not make.
