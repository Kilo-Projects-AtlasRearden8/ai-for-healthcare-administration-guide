# 01 Introduction: AI for Healthcare Administration

Plain English, step by step. No tech background needed.

## What AI is, in friendly terms

Artificial intelligence (AI) is software that can read, write, summarize, and
organize information in ways that feel human. You have probably already used
it: autocomplete in text messages, spam filters, or recommendation lists.

For healthcare administration, the most useful AI tools are:

- **Chat assistants** — you type a request, the AI writes a draft or answers a
  question (example: "Draft a friendly appointment reminder text message")
- **Document helpers** — summarize long documents, rewrite text in plainer
  language, fill in templates
- **Local AI** — models that run on your own computer instead of sending data
  to a company's servers (good for privacy-first workflows)

## What AI is NOT

- AI is not a doctor. It cannot diagnose, treat, or give medical advice.
- AI is not a lawyer. It cannot give legal advice or review contracts for you.
- AI is not a certified coder. It cannot tell you which billing codes to use.
- AI is not HIPAA-compliant by default. Most public AI tools are not safe for
  patient data.

## Choosing your first AI tool

Start with one tool and one task. Ask your compliance officer which tools are
approved at your clinic.

| Tool type | Good for | Watch out for |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Public chat AI (web-based) | Drafting emails, templates, practice examples | Never paste patient data |
| Clinic-approved AI with a BAA | Some admin drafting | Still confirm with compliance |
| Local AI (runs on your PC) | Drafting with sensitive-but-fake data | Setup takes more time |

**Rule of thumb:** if you would not print it and tape it to a public bulletin
board, do not paste it into a public AI tool.

## Privacy habits to start today

- Use made-up names and dates in every AI prompt. "Jane Example," born
  01/01/2000, seen 03/15/2026.
- Never include a real patient's name, MRN, insurance ID, or contact info.
- Start a new chat for each task instead of reusing one long conversation.
- Turn off "memory" or "chat history" features if your tool offers it and your
  clinic allows.

## Your first AI task (5 minutes)

Try this with fake data only:

1. Open your AI tool.
2. Type: "Write a friendly, one-paragraph appointment reminder for a dental
   cleaning. Include date, time, address, and a line asking the patient to
   call 555-0100 to confirm or reschedule. Keep it under 60 words. Use fake
   patient name Jamie Example."
3. Read the output. Fix anything that sounds off. That's it — you just used
   AI for admin work.
