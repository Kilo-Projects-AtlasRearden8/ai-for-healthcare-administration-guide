# 05 Compliance Reminders

Education only. This is not legal advice and not a HIPAA compliance program.
Your clinic's compliance officer and legal counsel have the final word.

## The big rule: never put patient data in AI

Protected Health Information (PHI) — anything that can identify a patient —
does not belong in public AI tools. No names, no birth dates, no MRNs, no
insurance IDs, no diagnosis details, no photos.

If you would not want it on a postcard, do not paste it into an AI chat.

## HIPAA basics (education only)

HIPAA is the federal law that protects patient health information. Key ideas:

- **Minimum necessary:** Only use or share the minimum amount of patient
  information needed to do the job.
- **Authorized access:** Only staff who need the information for treatment,
  payment, or operations should see it.
- **Safeguards:** Lock screens, shred paper, use secure networks, and don't
  discuss patients where others can hear.
- **Business Associate Agreement (BAA):** A contract required when a vendor
  handles PHI on your clinic's behalf. Most public AI tools will not sign a
  BAA — which is another reason not to use them with patient data.

## AI vendor questions to ask your compliance officer

Before using any AI tool at work:

1. Is this tool approved by the clinic?
2. Does the vendor sign a BAA?
3. Where is data stored, and who can see it?
4. Is my chat used to train the model? Can I turn that off?
5. Who do I report mistakes or accidents to?

If the answer to #2 is "no," treat the tool as unsafe for anything related to
real patients.

## Daily compliance habits

- Log out or lock your computer when you step away.
- Shred papers with patient information — never toss them in recycling.
- Use fake examples (Jane Example, 01/01/2000) when practicing with AI.
- Start a fresh AI chat for each task.
- Report suspected mistakes or data exposures to your compliance officer
  right away. Fast reporting limits harm.

## If something goes wrong

- Accidentally pasted patient info into AI? Tell your compliance officer
  immediately. Do not try to "delete" it yourself — you may not be able to.
- Unsure if a task is allowed? Ask before doing it, not after.
