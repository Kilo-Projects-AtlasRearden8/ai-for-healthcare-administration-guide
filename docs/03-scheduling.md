# 03 Scheduling: Appointments, Reminders, and Calendars

AI can help you draft reminder messages, organize scheduling rules, and reduce
no-shows — with you reviewing every message before it goes to a patient.

## What AI can help with

- Drafting appointment reminder texts, emails, and phone scripts
- Writing waitlist messages and cancellation policies
- Creating scheduling templates for different visit types (new patient,
  follow-up, physical, telehealth)
- Drafting scripts for front-desk phone conversations
- Summarizing scheduling policies into a one-page staff cheat sheet

## What to keep human

- Never let AI book, change, or cancel real appointments on its own.
- Never paste real patient names, phone numbers, or appointment details into
  an AI tool.
- A staff member must approve and send every reminder.

## Step-by-step: write an appointment reminder text

Fake patient: Jamie Example, appointment 04/10/2026 at 2:00 PM, Dr. Rivera,
123 Example Street, Suite 4. Callback line: 555-0100.

1. **Open a new chat** with your AI tool.
2. **Paste a prompt like this:**

   > "Draft a short, friendly appointment reminder text message for a clinic
   > patient. Use fake details only: patient Jamie Example, appointment
   > 04/10/2026 at 2:00 PM with Dr. Rivera at 123 Example Street, Suite 4.
   > Include: date, time, provider, address, 'please arrive 15 minutes early,'
   > 'bring your insurance card and photo ID,' and 'call 555-0100 to confirm,
   > reschedule, or cancel.' Keep it under 160 characters if possible."

3. **Review the output.** Does it sound like your clinic? Fix the tone.
4. **Copy into your scheduling/reminder system** with real patient details
   added by staff — never inside the AI chat.
5. **Send and log** the reminder per your clinic's policy.

## Reducing no-shows (non-AI tips that pair well)

- Send reminders 24–48 hours before the visit.
- Offer text, email, and phone options based on patient preference.
- Keep a waitlist so canceled slots can be refilled.
- Write your cancellation policy in plain language and post it at check-in.

## Sample staff cheat sheet (draft with AI, finalize with your team)

You can ask AI to draft a one-page cheat sheet:

> "Draft a one-page front-desk cheat sheet for scheduling: new patient visits
   are 60 minutes, follow-ups are 30 minutes, telehealth visits are 20
   minutes. Include the reminder script above, the cancellation policy, and
   what to do when a patient is late. Plain English, friendly tone."

Then print it and keep it at the front desk.
